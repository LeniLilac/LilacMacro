# Community conduct

LilacMacro welcomes careful, good-faith participation. Be respectful, keep technical disagreements about the work, and make space for people with different experience levels.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate, dox, or deliberately expose another person's credentials, private links, captures, diagnostics, or account information. Do not use project spaces to coordinate platform abuse, malware, anti-cheat bypasses, or access to systems you do not own or have permission to test.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or the project. Report conduct concerns privately to [lilithlilac000@gmail.com](mailto:lilithlilac000@gmail.com). Reports will be handled as privately as practical, subject to safety and legal obligations.
